Pond 2 · live

Four sensors, one calm reading of the pond.

AquaSense watches pond turbidity and the temperature inside two node enclosures (with an over-temperature alert on N1) from three ESP32 nodes, aggregates them at a cluster head, drives a two-LED status light at the sink and reports to this laptop over a self-built MAC and routing layer.

See the 17-step demo flow
